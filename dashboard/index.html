SKEMA PENGKABELAN VORTEX-X (ESP32)

ESP32 #1: TRANSMITTER (Audio I2S + Web)

Sensor PIR: GPIO 19 (D19)

Mic INMP441: BCLK 32 | WS 33 | SD 34 (3.3V)

Spk MAX98357A: BCLK 26 | LRC 25 | DIN 22 (5V)

Sensor DHT11: DATA GPIO 18 (D18) (VCC 3.3V)

Layar I2C (OLED/LCD): SDA G21 | SCL G23

ESP32 #2: RECEIVER (4 Servo Bawah)

Belakang Kiri (RL): GPIO 13 (D13)

Depan Kiri (FL): GPIO 14 (D14)

Belakang Kanan (RR): GPIO 18 (D18)

Depan Kanan (FR): GPIO 21 (D21)

Power Servo: 5V BEC + Common GND

LCD 16x2 I2C (Pada RECEIVER)

VCC: WAJIB 5V (VIN / BEC 5V)

GND: Common GND Receiver

SDA: GPIO 19 (D19) — Bukan G21!

SCL: GPIO 22 (D22)

Fungsi: Tampil data DHT, PIR, Mic & Mode RX

KOMUNIKASI WIRELESS

Web ↔ TX: Wi-Fi HTTP (REST API)

TX ↔ RX: ESP-NOW Auto-Pairing (< 5ms)
